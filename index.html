Questions

Are you legal and insured?

Yes. I hold a CAA Flyer ID and Operator ID and carry public liability insurance. I'm happy to share certificates before any job.

What happens if the weather is bad?

Drones can't fly safely in rain or strong wind. If the forecast is poor, we simply move the flight to the next good day at no extra cost.

Can you fly anywhere?

Most places, yes. Some areas near airports, prisons or busy events need permission or have restrictions. I check the airspace before every job and will tell you straight away if there's an issue.

How quickly will I get my photos and video?

Photos usually arrive within 48 hours and edited video within 5 working days, sent as a download link.

Can I use the footage on social media and in adverts?

Yes. You're free to use everything I deliver for your business, listing or event. I may share some shots in my own portfolio unless you'd rather I didn't.

Do you fly indoors?

Yes, with the Avata 2. Its protected propellers make it suited to smooth fly-throughs of homes, shops, gyms and venues.
